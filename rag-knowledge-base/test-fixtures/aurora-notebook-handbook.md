# Aurora Notebook — Owner's Handbook

Welcome to the Aurora Notebook family. This handbook covers everything you need to know about your device: setup, hardware specifications, warranty terms, support channels, and troubleshooting. Keep it somewhere accessible — the answers to most common questions live in these pages.

## Model Overview

The Aurora Notebook line launched in March 2025 with three configurations:

- **Aurora Notebook 13** (model code AN13-A1) — 13.4" display, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD. Starting price $1,299.
- **Aurora Notebook 15** (model code AN15-B2) — 15.6" display, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD. Starting price $1,799.
- **Aurora Notebook Pro** (model code ANP-C3) — 16.2" display, 64 GB RAM, 2 TB SSD, dedicated GPU. Starting price $2,899.

All three models share the same aluminum unibody chassis, magnetic charging port, and backlit keyboard. The Pro adds a haptic trackpad and a color-calibrated OLED panel.

## Setup

When you first power on your Aurora Notebook, the setup assistant will guide you through the following steps:

1. Choose your language and region.
2. Connect to Wi-Fi (Ethernet is also supported via the USB-C adapter included in the box).
3. Sign in with your Aurora account, or create one. Your account syncs settings, files, and installed applications across every Aurora device you own.
4. Set up biometric login using either the fingerprint sensor in the power button or facial recognition via the front-facing IR camera.
5. Enable full-disk encryption (recommended for all users, required for enterprise accounts).

Total setup time is typically under ten minutes on a broadband connection. First-run software updates may add an additional fifteen to thirty minutes.

## Warranty Coverage

Every Aurora Notebook ships with a **one-year limited warranty** that covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal use. The warranty period begins on the date of purchase, not the date of first activation, so keep your receipt.

Coverage includes:

- Display defects such as dead pixels above the industry threshold (more than five subpixels on the 13" model, more than eight on larger models).
- Battery capacity dropping below 80% of rated capacity within the first twelve months.
- Failure of the keyboard, trackpad, or ports under normal, non-liquid use.
- Boot failures caused by firmware or logic-board defects.

The warranty does not cover accidental damage (drops, spills, cracked screens), unauthorized repair attempts, or normal cosmetic wear. For accidental damage coverage, consider AuroraCare+, our extended protection plan starting at $199 for two years.

## Battery Care

The Aurora Notebook uses a lithium-polymer battery rated for approximately 1,000 full charge cycles before dropping to 80% of original capacity. A full charge cycle counts every time you consume 100% of the battery's capacity — so charging from 50% to 100% twice equals one full cycle.

To maximize battery lifespan:

- Avoid leaving the device plugged in at 100% for extended periods. The Aurora Notebook has a "battery health" mode in Settings that caps charging at 80% when the device is used primarily on AC power.
- Do not expose the device to temperatures above 35 °C or below 0 °C for extended periods.
- Every few weeks, allow the battery to drain to around 20% before recharging. This helps the battery gauge stay accurate.

Battery replacement is available through any Aurora Care Center. Out-of-warranty battery replacement costs $149 for the 13" model, $179 for the 15", and $229 for the Pro.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Device will not power on.** Connect the charger and wait at least fifteen minutes with the charging light visible. If the light does not appear, try a different USB-C cable and power adapter. If the light still does not appear, contact support — this is almost always a hardware issue that will be covered under warranty.

**Wi-Fi drops repeatedly.** Open Settings → Network → Wi-Fi Diagnostics and run the built-in test. In most cases this identifies whether the issue is with your network, your router, or the notebook's radio. Firmware updates released in the past six months have addressed the most common Wi-Fi stability issues on the AN15-B2 model in particular.

**Fingerprint sensor stops recognizing my finger.** This is usually caused by moisture, dry skin, or a scratch across the sensor. In Settings → Security → Fingerprint, delete the affected fingerprint entry and re-enroll. If enrollment fails repeatedly, the sensor may need replacement.

**Trackpad feels unresponsive after an update.** A small number of users have reported this on the Pro after installing the 2.4.0 firmware. Rolling back to 2.3.1 resolves the issue in every reported case. The 2.4.1 hotfix, released in October 2025, addresses the root cause and is the recommended long-term fix.

## Data and Privacy

Aurora does not sell your personal data. Diagnostic reports sent to Aurora are anonymized and can be disabled entirely under Settings → Privacy → Diagnostics. Sync data (settings, wallpapers, browser tabs) is end-to-end encrypted with a key derived from your account password; Aurora cannot decrypt it, and recovering your data if you forget your password requires the recovery key you generated during setup.

For enterprise deployments, Aurora offers a device-management console that allows IT administrators to enforce encryption, remotely wipe lost devices, and restrict application installation. Contact aurora-enterprise@example.com for a quote and integration documentation.

## Recycling

At the end of your device's useful life, Aurora accepts any Aurora-branded product for free recycling. Drop the device at any Aurora Care Center or request a prepaid shipping label at recycle.aurora-notebook.example. Devices returned for recycling are securely wiped, and reusable components are salvaged whenever possible.
